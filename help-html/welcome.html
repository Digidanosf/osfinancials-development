    osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 - Business Class

    After years of development and support for osFinancials and TurboCASH, the current model is no longer working effectively. To address this issue, the team is introducing osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 Business Class a new version with additional features and capabilities.

    While osFinancials 4 remains free to use, users seeking more advanced functionality can upgrade to osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 Business Class. This version is available free of charge for users with fewer than 500 transactions or documents. For those with more, a yearly contribution of 50 euros (excluding taxes) or 60 dollars will be required.

    For users with existing subscriptions for multi-user or retail POS, the license for osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 Business Class will be included as part of their existing subscription.

    Included in the registered version of osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 - Business Class

    osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 Subscription

    Process unlimited (more than 500) transactions and documents.

    Every time an unregistered version of osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 is started, the following confirmation message will be displayed:

    • This software is limited to 500 transactions or 500 documents. Please register and buy a license for osFinancials5.1/TurboCASH5-3.
      This copy of osFinancials/TurboCASH is not registered! Do you wish to register it now?

    Unregistered versions will allow you to process up to 500 transactions and/or documents. Once this limit is reached, osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 will not allow any processing of transactions, in batches and/or documents. 

    Once you have registered osFinancials5/TurboCASH5, you will receive an unlocking code and will be able to process further transactions and/or documents.

    Plugins included in the osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 Subscription

    Bank import 

    BankImport Plugin - 

    Licence : Included in osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 Subscription. 

    Documentation : Help topic.
    Documentation : osFinancials website - Bank import plugin - 
    Documentation : osFinancials website - Bank Reconciliations Plugin - 

    The Bank Import plugin included with the osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 Subscription is a handy tool for managing and processing the transactions in your bank and credit card accounts. You can download electronic bank statement files directly from your online banking services at your bank or financial institution. After that, you can use the Bank Import plugin to import these bank statements into your bank accounts. This process saves you a lot of time and effort as you do not have to manually enter and process the transactions of your bank accounts and credit card accounts in separate Payment journals and Receipt Journals. 

    Additionally, the Bank Import Plugin has the ability to use bank references to automatically assign transactions to the appropriate accounts, This automation not only saves time, but also reduces the risk of human error associated with manual data entry. Overall, using this Bank Import plugin is a smart choice for businesses or individuals who want to optimize their financial workflow within osFinancials5/TurboCASH5.

    Budget Plugin

    Budget Plugin - Manual - Shop - Help documentation 

    Licence : Included in osFinancials5 Subscription. 

    Effortlessly manage budgets for accounts, cost centres, and projects with the Budget Plugin, now included as a standard feature in the osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 Subscription. Access the Budgets option on the Reports ribbon for seamless integration.

    Key Features:

    • Flexible Budget Entry: Easily input budgets for accounts, cost centres, and projects directly within the osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 interface. Copy budget figures from the previous year of actual account balances.
    • Comprehensive Budgeting: Cover all aspects of financial planning by setting budgets for various entities, including accounts, cost centres, and projects.
    • Integrated Solution: Seamlessly integrate budget management into your overall financial workflow within osFinancials5/TurboCASH5.
    • Advanced Filtering and Pivot Options: Utilize advanced filtering and pivot functionalities to analyse budget data effectively and gain deeper insights into your financial performance.
    • Streamlined Processes: Simplify budgeting tasks and ensure accuracy by leveraging the intuitive interface and advanced features of the Budget Plugin.
    • Comprehensive Reporting: Generate budget listing reports (Trial balance, Income statement, Balance sheet, or Standard column balances report types) and budget performance reports (Budget vs Actual to compare the budget figures versus the actual posted transactions). Export reports to spreadsheets and utilize powerful pivot features for in-depth analysis. Print and export budgets in the T-Account viewer.

    Enhance your financial planning capabilities and streamline your budgeting processes with the Budget Plugin, empowering you to make informed decisions and drive financial success within your organisation.
