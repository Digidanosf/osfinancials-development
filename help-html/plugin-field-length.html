    Field length

    osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 Field length

    osFinancials Field length - Shop - Payment per request -

    With this tool you can change the field length of:

      • Product code
      • Product description
      • Debtor name
      • Creditor name
      • In the documents, and batch entries but also with the import of osCommerce.

    osFinancials offering field length customization as a paid service  for osFinancials5/TurboCASH5.  This is a valuable option for businesses that have very specific needs beyond the standard account structures. osFinancials goes even further by providing a dedicated "Field Length" where you can request and pay for specific field length adjustments.

    What it implies:

    • Flexibility for Unique Needs: Businesses often have unique chart of account structures, reporting requirements, or industry-specific categorizations that go beyond typical account numbering schemes. The Field Length Shop allows osFinancials to adapt to these non-standard needs, as well as other important fields.
    • Control and Precision: Customization gives businesses greater control over their financial data. They can tailor the system to match their exact processes and ensure that the software accurately reflects their financial activities.
    • Scalability: As a business grows or its needs evolve, the ability to adjust field lengths can be crucial for accommodating new data elements without requiring a complete system overhaul.
    • Professional Service: Offering this as a paid service through the Field Length indicates that osFinancials provides professional support and expertise to ensure that the customization is done correctly and efficiently. This can be especially valuable for businesses that lack in-house technical expertise.
    • Specific Customization Options: Through the Field Length Shop, osFinancials allows you to change the field length of key elements, including:
      • Product Code
      • Product Description
      • Debtor Name
      • Creditor Name
    • Comprehensive Application: These field length changes apply not only to documents and batch entries within osFinancials but also extend to data imported from osCommerce. This is a significant advantage for businesses using both platforms.
    • Potential Costs: While customization offers flexibility, it's important for businesses to understand the associated costs. They should get a clear understanding of the pricing structure and any potential ongoing maintenance fees before making a request through the Field Length Shop.

    Key Considerations for Businesses:

    • Clearly Define Requirements: Before requesting a field length change, businesses should clearly define their specific requirements. This includes identifying which fields need to be adjusted and the desired length for each field. The more specific you are, the smoother the process will be.
    • Evaluate Alternatives: Businesses should also consider whether there are alternative solutions, such as using prefixes, suffixes, or custom fields, that might achieve the desired result without requiring a formal request through the Field Length.
    • Get a Quote: Contact osFinancials directly through the Field Length process to get a detailed quote for the customization service, including any potential costs for implementation, testing, and ongoing support.
    • Document Changes: Ensure that all customizations are properly documented. This will be crucial for future upgrades, maintenance, and troubleshooting.

    By offering this customization service, and especially by providing the Field Length , osFinancials demonstrates a commitment to meeting the diverse needs of its osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 customers. It empowers businesses to leverage the software effectively, even with non-standard data requirements or integrations with other platforms like osCommerce.
