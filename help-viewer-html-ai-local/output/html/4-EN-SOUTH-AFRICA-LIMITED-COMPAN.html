    4-EN-SOUTH-AFRICA-LIMITED-COMPANY-IFRS-FULL

    Complete, non-truncated master data layout for the 4-EN-SOUTH-AFRICA-LIMITED COMPANY - Pty (Ltd) - IFRS (FULL) localized framework.

    This review updates outdated accounting terms (such as replacing "Debtor's/Creditor's control" with IFRS-compliant "Trade Receivables/Payables"), addresses structural issues (like removing the land amortization account, since land does not amortize under IAS 16), ensures every VAT sub-tier maps accurately to the physical SARS VAT201 return form layout, and validates all references against Full IFRS (IAS/IFRS rules) and the South African VAT Act 89 of 1991.
