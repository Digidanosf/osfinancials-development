    Critical Review & Alignment Notes

    While the chart is highly functional and complete, you need to address four subtle structural group discrepancies to maintain precise US GAAP balance sheet logic and layout compliance within your software reporting database:

    • G26000000 (OTHER CURRENT LIABILITIES): Currently mapped to SGROUP2 = CURRENT LIABILITIES, but its SGROUP1 is assigned to CURRENT LIABILITIES@Long-term debt current. This is a naming contradiction. It should be standardized to CURRENT LIABILITIES@Other current liabilities to keep it separated from your current debt maturities (G24200000).
    • G63000000, G63000100, G63000200 (CASH SURPLUS AND SHORTFALL): Currently grouped under SGROUP1 = EXPENSES@Non-cash expenses. This is technically incorrect; cash drawer shortfalls and petty cash discrepancies are liquid cash items, not non-cash accounting entries like depreciation. They should be mapped to EXPENSES@Administrative expenses.
    • G67000900 (Travel expenses): The SGROUP2 column is flagged as No group. This will cause the line to orphan on standard operating statement roll-ups. Assign it explicitly to EXPENSES to match its parent.
    • Member Guaranteed Payments Note: If this California LLC operates as a multi-member partnership and pays members for regular services, consider adding a distinct G62000000 sub-account explicitly titled Guaranteed Payments to Members (mapped to Form 1065, Line 10). This separates partner payments from regular employee payroll (Form 1065, Line 9) as required by the IRS.
