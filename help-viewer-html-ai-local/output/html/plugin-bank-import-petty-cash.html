    Bank import plugin - Petty cash transactions (Manual entry)

    Bank Import plugin Petty Cash transactions

    Since the Petty Cash account is included in the templates of osFinancials5/TurboCASH5 as a Bank account type. It  is recommended that Petty Cash accounts should be added or created as a Bank account type. Once the BankImport plugin is activated, the Petty cash payments batch and receipts batch will be combined into one “Batch entry” screen.

    Petty cash payments should be used as a bank account. If it is used as a normal General ledger – Balance sheet account, it could cause problems on the Tax Reports (Input VAT/GST/Sales tax). 

    Entering your petty cash payments (Manual entry)

    There are basically two (2) ways to capture the petty cash transactions:

    • Enter the transactions manually from your petty cash slips or petty cash vouchers.
    • Importing the transactions from a petty cash register as a Comma Separated Value (*.csv) file as a delimited file import.  

    For the purpose of this example, we assume that you have paid the following expenses from your petty cash:

    To enter your petty cash payment transactions:

    1. Go to the Default ribbon and select Cash/Bank entry . The "Bank statements options" screen will list your previous processed transactions.

    1. Select the Petty cash as the “Bank account” and click Open. 
    2. Enter the alias (batch name) in the “Change alias” field. In this example, enter ''PettyCash2403'' to indicate that the Petty cash of March 2024 was entered in this Petty cash payments batch.

    The alias option allows you to identify a batch by a unique name. You will be able to select the batch by alias or batch name if you need to reverse the posted batch or import a posted batch. 

    1. Click on the F10:Setup icon to set your batch up. This is a once-off action, unless your requirements change. The “Options for this batch” screen is displayed: 

    The contra account is by default set to the Bank account. 

    You cannot change this in the Payments journal or Petty cash Payments journal. 
    1. Once setup; click on the Advanced tab.        

    1. Leave all fields blank, and make sure that the default option, “Account lookup type”, is set to “All” for this exercise. 
    2. Once setup; click OK. You are now ready to record your first petty cash payment.
    3. Capture your petty cash vouchers from your petty cash voucher book or from your requisitions. Use your petty cash voucher number as the reference number.

    Posting the petty cash batch 

    Once you have completed entering these petty cash payments, and you are sure they are correct, you may proceed to finalise the batch. The recommended process, is as follows: 

    1. Click on the F8:List icon to print a list of the transactions in the batch.

    To include Tax (VAT/GST/Sales tax) amounts in the List batch - Unposted batch report,you need to balance the batch first. 

    To do this, you need to go to F9:Process and choose the Balance batch. 

    It will generate balancing entries and include the Tax (VAT/GST/Sales tax) amounts on the List batch - Unposted batch report . 

    It is recommended that the source documents (e.g. petty cash vouchers and/or invoices, slips, etc. received from suppliers, etc.) be attached to this List of the transactions and that it be retained for record and audit purposes.
    1. Click on the F6:Post icon to post (update) the batch to the ledger. Posting the transactions will automatically generate the necessary balancing entries. The “Batch entry” screen will automatically be closed. The “Bank statement options” screen is displayed: 

    1. All the imported and processed petty cash transactions, which are posted (updated) to the ledger, will be listed. 
    2. Click Close.

    T-Account view of transactions

    All processed (posted) transactions will be accumulated in the Ledger analyser. There are various ways in which the transactions may be viewed, exported and analysed.

    To access the T-Account view of the transactions:

    1. On the Reports ribbon, select Ledger analyser 1 or Ledger analyser 2. 
    2. Select “Trial balance” report type and click on the Report button.
    3. On the “Trial balance”, select the “B820-000 Petty cash” account. 
    4. Double-click on the selected account; or right-click and select Show details on the context menu.
    5. Click on the following to get specific views of transactions:
      • Batch id (e.g. 32 automatically generated). This will list only the transactions for a specific batch (journal). 
      • Account code (e.g. B820-000 Petty cash, G210-020 Motor vehicle - Maintenance G230-000 Stationery, T860-020 Input Tax Standard - 15%). This will list the transactions for a specific account.
      • Date - This will list the transactions for a specific date. If you double-click on a date, the “From date” and “To date” will be changed to the selected date.
